Module 4 • Smart Verification Workflow

7-Stage Hazard Verification Lifecycle

To prevent false warnings and panic, SAFEWAY employs a multi-tiered validation workflow combining computer vision, deduplication, and peer community consensus.

1
User Submits Report Includes photo, GPS coordinates, category, and timestamp.
2
AI-Assisted Vision Scan Extracts visual features (pothole depths, submerged pavements, debris bounds).
3
Duplicate Clustering Prevents redundant reports within 150m radius by merging confirmation counts.
4
Community Peer Verification Nearby drivers traveling past the location confirm or resolve the obstacle.
5
Dynamic Score Influence Verified reports dynamically elevate the road segment's risk score on live maps.
Caution Note: AI predictions are assistive tools, not automatic confirmed evidence. Final verification requires peer consensus or moderator sign-off.
